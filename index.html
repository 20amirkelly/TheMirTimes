New Supreme Court term begins with a climate change case seeking to hold energy companies liable

The Supreme Court heard oral arguments Monday in a landmark climate change case that could determine whether state and local governments can hold major fossil fuel companies financially liable for climate-related disasters. The lawsuit, brought by Boulder, Colorado, accuses Suncor Energy and ExxonMobil of a decades-long campaign of deception regarding the climate risks of their products. Boulder is seeking damages under state tort laws to cover the mounting local costs of extreme weather, including the devastating 2021 Marshall Fire. The energy giants countered that global greenhouse gas emissions inherently cross state lines and must be governed—and ultimately preempted—by federal law, rather than a patchwork of state courts.

New $211 Hodgson Vo-Tech builds in space for student mental health

The newly opened $217 million campus of Hodgson Vo-Tech High School is prioritizing student well-being with a dedicated mental health sanctuary built directly into its design. On Sept. 24, school officials cut the ribbon on "Sean's Room," a specialized space operated in partnership with the UnLocke the Light foundation. The room provides a safe, judgment-free environment where students can access free mental health resources, decompress, and connect with peer support. Its inclusion in the state-of-the-art, 287,000-square-foot facility underscores a growing educational trend of treating emotional wellness as a core component of modern school infrastructure.

The Pros and Cons of Enabling Wi-Fi Calling

As cellular dead zones continue to frustrate homeowners and office workers, experts are urging smartphone users to activate a simple, built-in solution: Wi-Fi calling. By routing voice calls and text messages through a local internet connection rather than a distant cell tower, the feature guarantees crystal-clear audio in notoriously spotty areas like basements and thick-walled buildings. Beyond eliminating dropped calls, enabling Wi-Fi calling can significantly extend battery life by preventing the device from constantly straining to find a weak cellular signal. The setting, located in the cellular or connection menus of nearly all modern smartphones, is free to use and can even help international travelers bypass steep roaming charges by keeping them connected over hotel Wi-Fi.

America must defend our digital infrastructure, here and overseas, to win the next war

Military strategists and cybersecurity experts are issuing a stark warning: the next major global conflict will be won or lost in cyberspace. Defense officials are increasingly emphasizing that securing American digital infrastructure from domestic power grids and financial systems to overseas subsea cables and cloud networks—must become the nation's top security priority. As adversaries develop sophisticated cyber capabilities designed to cripple critical systems before traditional combat even begins, experts argue that preemptive digital defense is no longer optional. To deter future aggression and secure victory in the 21st century, policymakers say the U.S. must seamlessly integrate its physical military might with an impenetrable global cyber shield.
